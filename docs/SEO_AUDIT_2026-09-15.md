# QuranPilot SEO follow-up — 15 September 2026

Reviewed live chapter, verse, directory and article HTML and the corresponding source. This follow-up improves content identification and discovery after the pagination and crawl-directive fixes.

## Live findings and changes

- Article pages (`/articles/understanding-surah-al-fatihah` and `/articles/when-was-islam-created-at-first`) advertised `og:type=website` and the generic `/opengraph-image`. Articles now use `og:type=article`, their existing publication date, and their own illustration for Open Graph and Twitter. Default previews for other routes are preserved. This improves shared-link presentation; it does not by itself guarantee search ranking gains.
- Article JSON-LD already credited QuranPilot as the organizational author, but lacked an identifying author URL. Added the organization URL, publisher logo, English language, article section, and free-access information. The visible byline now matches the existing author markup. No individual author, editorial review, credentials, or modification dates were invented.
- `/al-baqarah/255` had “Ayatul Kursi” in metadata but only “Al-Baqarah 2:255” in its H1. A shared heading now includes the familiar verse name and reference, including translated names where available.
- `/surahs` had no direct link to Ayatul Kursi. Added a visible featured-verse section linking to Ayatul Kursi, the Light Verse, and each of the final two verses of Al-Baqarah. Added the latter two existing verse routes to static generation and the sitemap in English, Urdu, Pashto and Persian: 627 total sitemap URLs, up from 619.
